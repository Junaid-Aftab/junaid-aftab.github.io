### Course Information:

**Instructor:** Junaid Aftab  
**Email:** [junaida@umich.edu](mailto:junaida@umich.edu)

**Location:** 1250 Undergraduate Sciences Building (USB)  
**Office Hours:** TBA.


**Description:**  Math 241 (Applied Linear Algebra) introduces the fundamental concepts of linear algebra, including systems of linear equations, matrix operations,  Euclidean vector spaces, linear independence, span, eigenvalues and eigenvectors. The course provides the foundation needed to understand a wide range of applications of linear algebra, such as linear programming,  linear differential equations and mathematical economics.

**Prerequisites:** Students registering for this class must have completed MATH 116, 156, 186, 276, or 296.

**Restrictions:**  No credit will be granted to students who have completed or are currently enrolled in MATH 217, 417, 419, or 420.

**Intended Audience:** The Math 214–215 sequence is designed for engineering students who need a stronger foundation in linear algebra and less emphasis on differential equations. It serves as an alternative to the Math 215–216 sequence and is not intended for mathematics majors. Students preparing for a mathematics degree should take Math 217 as their linear algebra course.

**Textbook:** The textbook is *Linear Algebra with Applications* by Otto Bretscher. The course will cover Chapters 1–3 and 5–8, excluding sections 5.5, 7.6 and 8.2. The textbook will be used for assigned readings, in-class problem discussions, and homework exercises.
